How Labubo knows what's happening

Labubo reads a plain little file called project-status.json from your project. Every time you finish a step, update that file and Labubo refreshes itself within a few seconds — no rebuild, no restart.

  1. Open project-status.json next to this file's folder.
  2. Mark a step "done", "active", "blocked" or "todo".
  3. Click Labubo — the panel is already updated.
